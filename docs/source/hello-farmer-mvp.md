78.
Conclusion
Hello
Farmer
addresses
a
fundamental
accessibility
problem
in
agricultural
information
services.
Ethiopia
already
has
telephone-based
agricultural
information
infrastructure.
The
challenge
is
making
that
infrastructure
easier
and
more
natural
for
farmers
to
use.
Traditional
IVR
systems
require
farmers
to
adapt
to
menus,
keypad
sequences,
registration
procedures,
and
rigid
information
categories.
Hello
Farmer
takes
a
different
approach.
The
farmer
speaks
naturally.
The
AI
understands
the
language.
The
system
identifies
the
agricultural
problem.
Farmer
context
is
extracted
dynamically.
Trusted
agricultural
information
is
retrieved.
Weather
information
is
considered
when
relevant.
The
AI
generates
grounded
guidance.
The
response
is
delivered
through
voice.
After
the
call,
the
farmer
can
receive
a
localized
summary.
When
an
agricultural
risk
is
detected,
the
system
can
proactively
send
a
warning.
For
smartphone
users,
the
same
intelligence
can
be
accessed
through
a
web
experience.
For
the
MVP,
Asterisk
and
Docker
provide
the
telephone
infrastructure
without
requiring
a
telecom
provider
or
production
8028
integration.
Free-tier
services
and
open-source
components
are
used
wherever
practical
so
that
the
complete
concept
can
be
developed
and
demonstrated
with
minimal
cost.
The
long-term
vision
is
to
create
an
agricultural
information
system
that
does
not
force
farmers
to
learn
complicated
technology.
Instead,
the
technology
learns
to
understand
the
farmer.
Hello
Farmer
makes
agricultural
information
conversational,
accessible,
multilingual,
contextual,
and
proactive.
