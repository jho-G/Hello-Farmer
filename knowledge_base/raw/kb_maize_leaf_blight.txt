Maize foliage can be affected by fungal foliar diseases during wet and humid periods. Symptoms often start on lower leaves as elongated grayish or brownish spots, which may expand along the leaf veins. Early recognition allows farmers to avoid working in fields when leaves are wet to limit spore spread.
