Vertisols (heavy black clay soils, walka / koticha) swell when wet and crack when dry. During heavy rains, water infiltration slows dramatically, causing waterlogging that can suffocate cereal seedlings. Broadbed and furrow techniques or traditional drainage furrows help channel excess water off the field without causing severe soil erosion.
