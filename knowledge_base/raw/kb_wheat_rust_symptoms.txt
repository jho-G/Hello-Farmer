Wheat yellow rust (stripe rust) appears as linear stripes of yellow to orange pustules on the upper surface of wheat leaves. The disease develops rapidly under cool, humid weather conditions in highland areas. Farmers should monitor fields regularly during tillering and heading stages. For chemical control decisions and fungicide selection, farmers must consult their local agricultural extension office or development agent immediately upon observation.
