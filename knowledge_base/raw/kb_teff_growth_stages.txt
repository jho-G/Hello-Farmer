Teff (Eragrostis tef) is a cereal crop sensitive to water conditions during its early vegetative cycle. When excess standing water accumulates in the root zone, the plants may show yellowing leaves and reduced vigor due to low soil aeration. Yellowing can also be caused by poor soil nutrient availability in poorly drained soils. 

Farmers observing yellow leaves should examine the field for signs of standing water. If water is pooling, creating surface drainage furrows can help remove excess surface water. For specific fertilizer amounts or treatment recommendations, consult the local development agent.
