# Title: Agrochemical Handling Safety Principles

# Language: am / om / en

[PLACEHOLDER NOTICE: This document contains only general safety principles. It contains no numbers, no dosage rates, no chemical names, and no schedule figures.]

All crop protection chemicals must be handled with extreme care. Never spray against the wind direction. Keep chemicals away from drinking water sources, livestock, and children. In the event of chemical contamination or suspected poisoning, seek immediate medical assistance at the nearest health center. Always consult the local agricultural development agent before purchasing or applying any pesticide product.
